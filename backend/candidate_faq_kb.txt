[CATEGORY: WORKING HOURS & WORK LOCATION POLICY]
Q1: What are HireFlow's official working hours?
A: HireFlow maintains flexible core working hours from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM (Monday through Friday). Team members can customize their start time between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM.

Q2: Does HireFlow support remote or hybrid work?
A: Yes! HireFlow operates on a Hybrid-First model. Engineers and team members are required to work in-office 2 days a week (Tuesday and Thursday) and can work remotely for the remaining 3 days. Full-remote accommodations are available for senior roles upon leadership approval.

Q3: Is overtime paid at HireFlow?
A: Regular salaried employees receive compensatory time-off (Comp-Off) for pre-approved weekend or holiday deployment activities. Contract personnel are paid 1.5x hourly rate for hours exceeding 40 hours per week.


[CATEGORY: LEAVE & VACATION POLICY]
Q4: What is HireFlow's annual paid leave policy?
A: Full-time employees receive 24 days of Paid Leave per year (earned at 2 days per month), 10 national holidays, 12 Casual/Sick leaves, and 3 Optional Floating Holidays.

Q5: What is the parental leave duration?
A: Primary caregivers receive 26 weeks of fully paid Maternity Leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of fully paid Paternity Leave.


[CATEGORY: RECRUITMENT PIPELINE & INTERVIEW PROCESS]
Q6: How does HireFlow's AI Candidate Screening process work?
A: Applications are evaluated by HireFlow's 9-Agent LangGraph AI pipeline. The system analyzes skill relevance, project complexity, and experience match directly against the job description while masking PII (name, gender, age) to guarantee zero-bias fair scoring.

Q7: What happens after an interview is scheduled?
A: Candidates receive an automated email confirmation containing a live Google Meet link. Candidates can also view, join, or request rescheduling directly from their HireFlow Candidate Dashboard under "My Applications".

Q8: How long does it take to receive an offer letter after the final interview?
A: Offer decisions are generated within 48 to 72 hours following the final interview round. Once generated by HR, the official offer letter (.docx / .txt) is available for e-signature directly inside the candidate portal.


[CATEGORY: HARDWARE & REMOTE WORK STIPEND]
Q9: Does HireFlow provide laptops and work devices?
A: Yes. All engineering and product team members receive a brand new Apple MacBook Pro (M-Series) or high-spec Dell XPS laptop, along with a $500 home-office setup stipend.

Q10: Is internet and learning reimbursement provided?
A: HireFlow provides a $50/month Wi-Fi allowance and a $1,000 annual AI Learning & Professional Conference stipend for books, online courses, and certifications.
